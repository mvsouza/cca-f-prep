The 100-Tool Nightmare: Dynamic Discovery

Registering 50+ tools directly into Claude degrades precision and explodes prompt token costs. Here is the enterprise architectural fix.

🏗️ Architectural Pattern:

Use a Meta-Tool Discovery registry. Provide Claude with a single search_tools interface. It retrieves only the 2 required tool schemas at runtime, preventing capability bloat and cutting token spend by up to 90%.

⭐ Exam Golden Takeaway: Whenever a scenario mentions routing errors from too many tools, the answer is always dynamic tool discovery or hierarchical subagents—never increasing max tokens.
